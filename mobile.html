I've noticed that many people are quick to tell you what they don't eat.

But when I ask a simple question, "Have you ever tried it?" the answer is often no.

That makes me wonder: how can you know you don't like something you've never tasted?

Some of life's best experiences begin with a single bite and an open mind.

So before you say no, take a chance.
